1. Who is responsible

DASON is operated by DASON, CNPJ 61.076.954/0001-72 (Brazil), based in Madrid, Spain. You can reach us at contactdasontech@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described below.

2. What we collect

When you send a request through our form: your name, email, company, country, your business Instagram handle (optional), what you want to automate and the message you write.

When you message us on Instagram, Meta processes that conversation under its own terms and we see what you send.

This site sets no cookies. Your browser stores your language choice locally so the page remembers it. Our hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to keep the site running.

We measure visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly tool that sets no cookies and does not track you across sites. It counts page views, the page you came from, your country, your browser and screen size, and stores no personal profile of you.

3. Why we use it and on what basis

To answer your request and, if you ask, prepare a proposal. The basis is taking steps at your request before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); LGPD Art. 7, V) and our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); LGPD Art. 7, IX). Where the law requires consent, we ask for it separately.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

4. Who receives it

Service providers that help us run the site and answer you: website hosting (GitHub Pages), form handling (Formspree) and email (Google Gmail). They process data on our behalf and may only use it for that purpose.

5. Transfers outside your country

Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area or Brazil. When that happens we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

6. How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to a project: up to 24 months, then deleted. Client records: for the length of the contract and the period the law requires us to keep accounting and tax records. You can ask us to delete your data earlier.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, to object to its use and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to contactdasontech@gmail.com and we will answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es); in Brazil, the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (gov.br/anpd); or the authority in your own country.

8. Third-party content

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is shared with Google when the page opens. Links to Instagram and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

9. Security and minors

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. This site is not aimed at people under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
