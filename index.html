What kind of work can you automate?
Repetitive work with clear rules: copying data, answering common questions, issuing documents, building reports, moving information between tools.

      
Do you replace people?
No. We remove the repetitive part of the work so people can do the part that needs judgment.

      
How much does it cost and how long does it take?
It depends on the scope. After we understand your process you receive a written proposal with price and deliverables before work begins.

      
Where are you based and who can hire you?
DASON is remote-first and works with companies in any country, in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

      
Which tools do you work with?
With the ones you already use: spreadsheets, email, CRMs, accounting, e-commerce and messaging. We start from your setup.
